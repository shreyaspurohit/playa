Playa Camps Privacy Policy

Effective August 13, 2026

Playa Camps is a private, non-commercial planning application. The app itself has no advertising, no per-user analytics, and no accounts, and sets no cookies or tracking scripts of its own. It is served through GitHub Pages and Cloudflare, which process ordinary request metadata (such as IP addresses) to deliver and protect the site and expose aggregate traffic statistics; some Cloudflare services may set their own cookies. Most app data stays in your browser. Dropbox backup and restore is optional and begins only when you choose to connect Dropbox.

Information stored by Dropbox sync

When you connect Dropbox, Playa Camps may read and write one readable JSON backup in its dedicated Dropbox App folder. The backup can contain your nickname; IDs for favorites and plans; hidden schedule days; home-camp and meet-spot selections; imported friends’ nicknames and shared selections; and app preferences such as theme, distance unit, and map layers.

The backup does not contain the Playa Camps site password, Dropbox credentials, live or simulated GPS location, simulated time, access-tier keys, camp descriptions, or event descriptions.

How the information is used

The app uses the backup only to restore your state and merge changes across browsers and devices you connect. Processing occurs in your browser. Playa Camps does not operate a server that receives the backup, and it does not sell, advertise with, profile, or share the backup with other users.

Dropbox access

Playa Camps requests only Dropbox file-content read and write permissions for its App folder. It cannot browse or access files elsewhere in your Dropbox. Dropbox handles authentication and stores the backup under its own terms and privacy policy. Dropbox access and refresh tokens are encrypted on your device with a non-extractable browser key.

Your choices and retention

You can use Playa Camps without Dropbox. Disconnecting revokes this device’s connection but deliberately leaves the backup available for later restoration. To remove the cloud copy, delete the Playa Camps Sync App folder from Dropbox. You can also revoke the app from your Dropbox account settings. Clearing local Playa Camps data removes the local connection and local planning state but does not delete the Dropbox backup.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app’s data practices change. The operator contact for questions or privacy requests is available in the Playa Camps About & disclaimer panel.
